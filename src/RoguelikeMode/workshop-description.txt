[b]⚠ EXPERIMENTAL (v0.1)[/b] — a brand-new game mode under active development. It never touches your campaign save slots, but expect rough edges and balance swings between versions.

A pure roguelike mode for Quasimorph: no ship, no campaign, no safety net. Start with empty pockets on floor 1, scavenge everything from the corpses and lockers around you, and ride the elevators down through ten escalating floors. The [b]GOLDEN KEYCARD[/b] waits at the bottom — seize it, call the evacuation, and reach an elevator to win. Die once and the run is over.

[h2]The Dive[/h2]
One new main-menu button opens the mode's own screen:
[list]
[*]Pick [b]DAILY DIVE[/b] or [b]RANDOM DIVE[/b], a difficulty (Easy / Normal / Hard), and one of three operators — hover any operator to inspect their full stats and perks
[*]The [b]DIVE LOG[/b] tracks your last run and your best scores
[*]Quitting mid-run is safe: [b]RESUME DESCENT[/b] restarts you at the top of the floor you were on
[/list]

[h2]The descent[/h2]
[list]
[*]Ten procedurally generated floors, each with its own biome, enemies and loot — the next elevator ride is always a surprise
[*]Enemy density, gear tech level and map size all escalate with depth. Elevators only go down
[*]On Easy and Normal a small supply cache waits at each floor's entrance
[*]Class chips found in the field can be learned on the spot — right-click one to retrain your operator mid-run
[*]Tutorial popups are silenced during runs; achievements are disabled so runs can't farm them
[/list]

[h2]The Daily Dive[/h2]
The daily run is seeded from the UTC date: [b]everyone in the world gets the same ten floors, the same enemies, the same loot and the same three operators to choose from[/b]. Same day, same tower — compare scores with friends. Re-rolling is impossible by design. An online daily ladder is in development.

[h2]Scoring[/h2]
Runs are scored on depth reached, kills, victory and damage taken, with a difficulty multiplier. Your best runs are kept locally and shown in the DIVE LOG. For fair comparisons (and the future ladder), play with this mod solo — the run summary tells you when other mods were active.

[h2]Saves & compatibility[/h2]
Roguelike runs use no save slots at all — your campaign saves are never touched, written to, or read. Run state and high scores live in separate [i]roguelike_*.dat[/i] files, so the mod can be removed at any time without harming a campaign. Built on the game's own mission, generation and difficulty systems with a small set of targeted Harmony patches.
